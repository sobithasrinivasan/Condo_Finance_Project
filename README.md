4.1 In Scope
•	Ingesting vendor invoices from a dedicated email account
•	Managing a list of vendors and their invoice/payment history
•	Uploading and processing bank statements and other financial documents
•	Automated bank reconciliation between statements and recorded invoices/deposits
•	A dashboard summarizing year-to-date (YTD) deposits and financial position
•	Monthly and quarterly financial reports
•	A simple user interface for board members to review and manage the above
